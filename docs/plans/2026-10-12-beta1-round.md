## Rules every lane inherits

- Plans are RULED; a design question not covered by a plan is a `task challenge`, ruled by the lead; implementation judgment within the plan is the implementer's (logged as notes).
- Measurements are serialised by `bench:host-lock` (bb rule `rule:bench-host-lock`); heavy builds pause while someone else holds it. Never kill a PID you did not spawn.
- Rust edits → rebuild the addon before JS tests. After a merge into `beta1`: `bun install --frozen-lockfile` in that worktree. Report regen (`m2-exit`, battery) with a fresh addon.
- Human-facing text in Thai or English; inter-agent messages in English.
- READY note shape: `READY @<sha> — <test counts> — <anything the reviewer must know>`. Reviewer answers `REVIEW-PASS @<sha>` or `REVIEW-FAIL @<sha> <findings>` on the task. Coordinator hands the lead `MERGE-READY <slug> @<sha>`.
